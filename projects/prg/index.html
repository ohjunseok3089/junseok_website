Real Time Open-Vocabulary Referential Gaze Control for Listening Robots

Dong Won Lee1, Junseok Oh2, Sarah Gillet1, Chaitanya Ahuja3, Andrea Tagliabue4, Yoon Kim5, Louis-Philippe Morency3, Cynthia Breazeal1, Hae Won Park1
1MIT Media Lab  •   2University of Pennsylvania  •   3Carnegie Mellon University
4Amazon Robotics  •   5MIT EECS
In Submission

Overview of our real-time open-vocabulary referential gaze control pipeline for listening robots.

Abstract

While listening, a robot must infer from live streaming speech and visual context whether to shift gaze, what to look at, and where the referent is, within the timing constraints of natural interaction. We propose an approach which combines a multimodal foundation model and an object segmentation model for real time open vocabulary referential gaze control. To achieve fast inference without sacrificing performance, we distill a slow but capable teacher into a fast student using a novel privileged information distillation method with human egocentric videos. Across held-out egocentric data and a new out-of-distribution panoramic benchmark, our model significantly improves performance over the base student and matches that of the teacher. We measure inference time across edge-to-cloud hardware and observe that our trained model has significantly lower inference time and memory requirements. In a video-based human-subject study, our approach improves perceived message understanding, behavioral interdependence, referential attention, and competence. These results demonstrate a practical path toward real-time, open-vocabulary referential gaze control.

Overview & Contributions

Multimodal Foundation Model & Segmentation

Combines streaming speech understanding (e.g., Gemma-4) and open-vocabulary object segmentation (e.g., SAM3) to anticipate referential gaze shifts toward indirect and direct targets.

Privileged Information Distillation

Distills a slow but high-capacity teacher into a fast student network using egocentric video supervision, matching teacher accuracy while dramatically slashing compute latency.

Real-Time Edge Deployment

Optimized for edge-to-cloud hardware to ensure latency meets natural interaction constraints (< ~400ms after noun onset) on physical robotic platforms.

Human-Subject Validation

Demonstrates statistically significant improvements in perceived message understanding, behavioral interdependence, referential attention, and robot competence in user studies.
